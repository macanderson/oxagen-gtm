Workforce management for autonomous agents

See which agent spent what, and on whose behalf.

Your customer's finance team will ask three things about an Oxagen bill: what the unit is, who incurred each one, and whether the invoice adds up. This brief gives you the answer to each, with the figures, the record behind them, and the words to use.

The short answer

Two meters, and they share no balance

Oxagen charges for the governance it does, not for the model tokens your customer's agents consume. There are two meters.

Governed action units (GAUs)

One governed action is one call routed through Oxagen that it checked and recorded: a tool call a rule allowed, an approval a person resolved, a capability an agent used. Each costs one unit. A plan includes a monthly allowance, and units beyond it are bought in blocks or invoiced at the contracted rate.

Usage credits for the in-app assistant

The assistant inside the Oxagen app runs on a model Oxagen pays for, unless the customer brings their own key. Its model cost is drawn from prepaid credits at cost, with no markup. One credit is one cent.

Why this matters to a buyer. The customer already pays their model provider directly. A vendor that took a percentage of that bill would earn more when the customer's agents burned more tokens. Oxagen's price moves with the number of governed actions, which is the work Oxagen does.

The unit

What counts as a governed action

A governed action is a call Oxagen admitted, answered with a rule, and recorded. The table below is the rule the meter applies, in the words you can give a customer.

Scope. The meter counts governed calls, the ones routed through Oxagen. An agent the customer has not wrapped, or a call it makes outside Oxagen, is neither governed nor billed. Say "for actions routed through Oxagen", never "every call".

Payment

How customers pay

Prepaid by card

Free, Build and Scale by default. When the month's units run out, Oxagen charges the saved card for one or more 5,000-unit blocks at the plan's rate and the agents keep working. The customer can also buy blocks at any time. With automatic top-up switched off, governed calls pause until the customer buys more or the month renews.

Invoice billing

For Scale and Enterprise, on approval. Governed calls are never paused for running past the allowance. Overage is invoiced at the contracted rate at month end. If overage reaches the agreed threshold mid-month (100,000 units by default), Oxagen issues an interim invoice for exactly that amount and keeps counting.

Enterprise prepaid order

One invoice, paid in advance, with up to three lines: the platform licence for the contract period, prepaid governed action units at the contracted rate, and prepaid usage credits for the in-app assistant. The invoice carries the agreement reference and the customer's PO number. Units and credits are granted when the invoice is paid.

The enterprise process, step by step

  1. Sign the agreement. It sets the rate per 1,000 governed actions, the included units each month, the block size and the currency.
  2. Oxagen records the terms. Billing operations enters them against the customer's organization with the agreement reference. From then on every monthly balance, invoice and statement uses them.
  3. Oxagen issues the prepaid invoice. Licence, prepaid units and prepaid assistant credits on one invoice, net 30 by default, with the agreement and PO number printed on it.
  4. The customer pays, and the balances appear. The prepaid units join the month's allowance and carry forward. The credits appear on the Billing page. Where the agreement allows, Oxagen can grant them when the invoice is issued instead.
  5. The customer tops up at any time. By card from the Billing page, or on another prepaid invoice from Oxagen. Overage beyond prepaid units is invoiced at the contracted rate.
  6. Finance closes the period with a statement. Any week, month, quarter or year, or any custom period longer than two days. See below.

The in-app assistant

Assistant usage is charged at cost, from prepaid credits

The assistant in the Oxagen app answers operators' questions and can act through the tools in their mandate. Its usage is charged on the second meter.

  • What it costs. Each model call the assistant makes is priced from Oxagen's published model rate card, with no markup, and drawn from the organization's usage credits. One credit is one cent.
  • Where the credits come from. Every new organization starts with $5. Build and Scale include a monthly credit allowance. Credit packs can be bought by card at any time, and enterprise customers prepay credits on their prepaid order.
  • Tools the assistant uses. When the assistant calls a governed tool on someone's behalf, that call is a governed action and costs one unit, like any other governed call.
  • Bring your own key. If the customer connects their own model key, the assistant's model cost goes to their provider bill and nothing is drawn from credits.
  • When credits run out. The assistant says so and points to the Billing page. It resumes when credits are added. A monthly cap on assistant spend applies too: $20 by default, set per agreement for enterprise customers.

Keep the meters apart. Credits never buy governed action units, and a block of units never adds credits. A customer who asks "can we use our credits for tool calls?" gets a plain no, and the reason: the two meters price different things.

Attribution

Every unit names what incurred it

Oxagen writes one ledger row for every billed governed action, in the same database transaction that counts it against the month's balance. So the units on an invoice and the rows behind it can be added up and compared, and they agree.

The agent

The registered agent that made the call, by name.

The operator

The person accountable for the run: the one who started it or authorised the agent.

The workspace

The governance partition, so costs roll up by team or project.

The tool call

The tool or capability, the MCP server, the harness, the run and the tool-call id.

Each row also records when the action happened and when it was billed. A field the source did not know stays empty. Oxagen never fills one in with a guess, because a guessed agent or run would put a charge against something that did not incur it.

Retries and re-sends. Every action carries a key taken from the tool call it answers. If an agent retries a tool call, or a laptop re-sends a batch of recorded tool calls after going offline, the second copy finds the first on the ledger and adds nothing.

Statements and invoices

A statement for the period finance closes

An Owner, an Admin or a Billing member can produce a statement for a week, a month, a quarter or a year, or for any custom period longer than two days. Oxagen's billing operations can produce the same statement for any customer. It comes as a printable document or as a spreadsheet file with one row per governed action.

What a statement shows

The terms in force and the agreement reference. Governed action units by agent, operator, workspace, tool and source, with a daily series. The month balances the period touches: included, bought, carried and used. Every purchase, top-up, prepaid order and invoice in the period. Usage credits: opening balance, grants, assistant consumption and closing balance.

How it reconciles

Add the itemised rows for a month and you get the units the month's balance counted. Subtract the included and bought units and you get the overage quantity on the invoice. Multiply by the contracted rate and you get the invoice line. Statements count an action in the period it was billed, so a statement and the invoices for the same period always cover the same units.

Questions you will hear

Answers you can give

Do you mark up our model bill?

No. The model spend of your own agents is reported in full, per run and per agent, and billed at $0.00. The in-app assistant's model cost is charged at cost from credits, with no markup.

If an agent retries a tool call, do we pay twice?

No. Each governed action carries a key from the tool call it answers. A retry or a re-sent batch finds the first copy and bills nothing.

Are denied calls charged?

No. A denial, a failure and an internal step cost nothing. A stricter policy never raises the bill.

Can we see which team, agent or person spent the units?

Yes. Every unit has a ledger row naming the agent, the operator, the workspace and the tool call. The statement groups by each of them, and the spreadsheet file lists every row.

Can finance get a statement for our fiscal quarter?

Yes. Any week, month, quarter or year, or any custom period longer than two days.

What happens if we run out of units in the middle of the month?

On a card plan, Oxagen tops up automatically in blocks unless you switch that off. On invoice billing, nothing pauses and the overage is invoiced at your contracted rate. A developer's laptop keeps recording and deciding tool calls from its cached rules either way.

Can we cap spend?

On a card plan, switch off automatic top-up and governed calls pause at the end of the allowance. Owners can set spend budgets for the organization or a workspace. Assistant spend has its own monthly cap.

We use Cursor. Is that covered?

Cursor's tool calls are governed and count like any other wrapped harness. Cursor's own model calls do not pass through Oxagen, so their model spend is not reported.

Do unused units roll over?

Bought units, by block or prepaid order, carry forward until used. The monthly allowance resets each month.

For the sales team

What to say, and what not to

Say

  • "For actions routed through Oxagen" and "governed calls".
  • "A rule allowed it", "a rule denied it", "routed to a person".
  • "The model spend of your agents is reported and billed at $0.00."
  • "Every unit on the invoice has a row naming the agent, the operator and the tool call."
  • "Quote from the pilot statement" for coding-heavy customers.

Do not say

  • "Every call" or "every run" without saying routed through Oxagen.
  • "You'll save money" or "costs less". There is no measured workload behind it.
  • "Unlimited". Enterprise is never paused, and it is still invoiced.
  • "Session", "trace", "execution" or "invocation". Say run, tool call or governed action.
  • Any rate that is not on the rate card or in the customer's agreement.

The one-paragraph answer

Oxagen charges per governed action: each call routed through Oxagen that a rule checked and Oxagen recorded, such as an allowed tool call. Denials, failures and retries cost nothing, and the model spend of your own agents is reported and billed at $0.00. Every unit on your invoice has a ledger row naming the agent, the operator, the workspace and the tool call, and you can pull a statement for any week, month, quarter, year or custom period.

Figures are the published rates as of 23 Sep 2026. A customer's agreement overrides them. Wrapped-harness and external tool-call billing, the per-action ledger, statements and prepaid orders ship with oxagen pull request 3911. Confirm it has shipped before promising a date to a customer.
